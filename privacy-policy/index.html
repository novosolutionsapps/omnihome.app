Health and Fitness Data

With your explicit permission, the Application can display health and fitness information — such as your daily step count, sleep data, and similar activity and wellness metrics — within the Application's home screen feed. This data is accessed from Android's Health Connect platform and/or your device's sensors only after you grant the relevant permissions.

All health and fitness data is processed entirely on your device and never leaves it. Specifically:

  • On-device, ephemeral processing only: health and fitness data is read and held in memory only for as long as necessary to display it to you. The Application does not write this data to persistent storage, and it is discarded as soon as it is no longer needed for display.
  • No collection or transmission: health and fitness data is never transmitted off your device and is never uploaded to our servers or to any service provider or other third party.
  • No sharing or sale: we do not share, sell, or otherwise disclose your health and fitness data to anyone.
  • No advertising or analytics use: health and fitness data is never used for advertising, marketing, analytics, profiling, or any purpose other than displaying it to you within the Application.

Access to health and fitness data is optional and is not required to use the Application. You can revoke the Application's access at any time through Health Connect or your device's settings. Because the Application does not store health and fitness data, revoking access immediately ends all processing, and there is no retained health data to delete.

Our access to and use of data received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16558241), including its Limited Use requirements.

These health and fitness restrictions prevail over every general data-use, sharing, aggregation, anonymization, commercial licensing, or AI provision in this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service. We do not use health or fitness data or derivatives of that data for AI training or commercial datasets, including in aggregated or de-identified form.
